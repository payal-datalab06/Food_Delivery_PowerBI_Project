# 🍔 Food Delivery Performance Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze food delivery operations, revenue performance, customer purchasing patterns, and delivery efficiency.

The dashboard provides insights into order trends, restaurant performance, payment preferences, customer demographics, and delivery partner performance.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze total orders and revenue performance.
- Evaluate delivery, cancellation, and delay rates.
- Identify restaurant categories generating the highest revenue.
- Understand customer age-group distribution.
- Analyze payment method preferences.
- Evaluate delivery time and distance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overall Order & Revenue Analysis
- Total orders and revenue.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | 2,600 |

| Delivery Rate | 34.42% |

| Average Delivery Time | 51.75 |
| Average Delivery Rating | 3.75 |

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Total revenue reached ₹1.74M across 2,600 orders.
- Bakery generated the highest restaurant-category revenue at approximately 308K.
- UPI was the most frequently used payment method.
- Delivered orders accounted for 34.42% of total orders.
- Average delivery time was 51.75 minutes.
- Customers aged 36–45 represented the largest age group by order count.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Investigate the high cancellation and delay rates.
- Improve delivery partner performance and operational efficiency.
- Analyze restaurant categories with lower revenue.
- Strengthen customer engagement across different age groups.
- Encourage convenient digital payment options.
